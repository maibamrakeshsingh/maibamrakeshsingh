## 👨‍🔬 About Me

I am **Maibam Rakesh Singh**, a mathematician and statistician interested in the intersection of **mathematical theory, statistical inference, computational methods, and complex systems**.

My research interests span probability, stochastic processes, mathematical statistics, scientific machine learning, random matrix theory, financial mathematics, statistical physics, and complex network inference.

I am particularly interested in developing **mathematical and computational frameworks for understanding uncertainty, collective behaviour, nonlinear dynamics, and complex adaptive systems**.
